About Us

Welcome to TranscribeAI, the ultimate solution for fast, secure, and accurate audio-to-text transcription. We built this tool with a singular focus: Privacy.

Traditional transcription services require you to upload your sensitive audio files to remote servers, exposing your data to potential breaches and third-party monitoring. TranscribeAI flips the script by utilizing cutting-edge WebAssembly and in-browser AI technology.

Our tool runs complex machine learning models (powered by Transformers.js) directly on your device. This means your audio files never leave your computer, ensuring 100% confidentiality.

Whether you are a journalist protecting a source, a student recording a lecture, or a professional documenting a meeting, TranscribeAI is here to serve you—completely free of charge.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

At TranscribeAI, your privacy is our core product feature. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle your data.

1. Data Processing

All audio processing and transcription are performed locally on your device within your web browser. We do not upload, transmit, or store your audio files or the resulting text on our servers. You maintain complete ownership and control over your data.

2. Information Collection

We do not collect personal information. We may use standard web analytics tools to monitor general website traffic (such as page views and browser types) to improve user experience. This data is fully anonymized.

3. Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
